## The Impact of Oral Ties

### Why Restrictions Are Harmful to Airway Health

Tongue ties, lip ties, and buccal ties can severely impact airway health by restricting proper tongue movement. When the tongue is tethered too tightly, it cannot rest against the roof of the mouth as it should, leading to compromised airflow and increased reliance on mouth breathing. This contributes to dry mouth, altered facial development, and poor-quality sleep. In more severe cases, a tongue tie may partially obstruct the airway, exacerbating issues like sleep apnea and upper airway resistance syndrome.

**Benefits for Children**

### Promoting Healthy Growth

In children, oral ties can impact jaw and airway development, leading to a narrower airway that is more prone to collapse as they grow. This increases the risk of snoring, sleep apnea, and related issues. By addressing tongue ties, lip ties, and buccal ties early through frenectomy or functional frenuloplasty, we can support better tongue posture, promote healthy nasal breathing, and ensure proper growth and development.

**Benefits for Adults**

### Improving Breathing and Sleep

Adults with tongue ties, lip ties, or buccal ties often experience symptoms such as mouth breathing, snoring, jaw pain, and poor sleep. By undergoing a frenectomy or functional frenuloplasty, adults can achieve better tongue mobility, improved airway function, and relief from these common issues. Our approach focuses on restoring optimal tongue and airway function for better breathing, deeper sleep, and enhanced overall health.
